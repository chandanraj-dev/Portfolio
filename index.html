Frequently Asked Questions

A few things clients usually ask before we start.

How long does a typical project take?

Most landing pages and portfolio sites take 3-7 days, while larger business websites can take 1-3 weeks depending on scope and content readiness.

How do we communicate during the project?

I keep clients updated over email, Telegram or WhatsApp, with regular progress updates so you're never left wondering where things stand.

Do you offer revisions?

Yes, reasonable revisions are included until you're happy with the final result, as agreed at the start of the project.

What do you need from me to get started?

A short brief about your goals, any content or branding you already have (logo, text, images), and examples of sites you like.
